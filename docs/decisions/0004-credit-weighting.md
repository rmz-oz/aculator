# 4. LO to PO on a 1-5 scale, courses weighted by ECTS

Two weights, two layers.

Inside a course, each LO is tied to a PO with a contribution level from 1 to 5, as in the guidelines (p. 7) and in the
Bologna course pages. The course PO score is the level weighted mean of its LO scores. Only the ratio matters, so the
Elif example with levels 2 and 1 gives the same PO1 = 85.0 as weights 1.0 and 0.5.

Across courses, a student's PO score is weighted by the ECTS stored on each
course offering. When an offering is created, it copies the course's catalog
ECTS. Later catalog edits affect future offerings, while existing offerings
retain their stored ECTS. Only the head of department can edit an existing
offering's ECTS. Kaplan Berkaya et al. (2025) also use credit weighting in a
MÜDEK accredited program with our 11 POs.
