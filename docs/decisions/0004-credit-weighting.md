# 4. LO to PO on a 1-5 scale, courses weighted by ECTS

Two weights, two layers.

Inside a course, each LO is tied to a PO with a contribution level from 1 to 5, as in the guidelines (p. 7) and in the
Bologna course pages. The course PO score is the level weighted mean of its LO scores. Only the ratio matters, so the
Elif example with levels 2 and 1 gives the same PO1 = 85.0 as weights 1.0 and 0.5.

Across courses, a student's PO score is the ECTS weighted mean of the course PO scores. Credits are already on every
course, so nobody enters extra weights and a heavier course counts more. Kaplan Berkaya et al. (2025) do the same in a
MÜDEK accredited program with our 11 POs. The credit is a field on the course, so a department can override it.
